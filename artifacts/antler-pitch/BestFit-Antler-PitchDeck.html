Core thesis

Online thrifting breaks when inventory is unique, sizing is inconsistent, and fit confidence is low. BestFit solves all three by pairing resale liquidity with a measurement system built for apparel.

From day zero

Built as a venture-scale wedge: start with a thrift marketplace, grow into a cross-brand fit data layer.

The Problem

Sizing guesswork breaks online thrift and leaves value stranded on both sides of the market.

Secondhand apparel is especially hard to buy online because every item is one-off, labels are unreliable, and shoppers cannot trust whether a garment will actually fit.

01

Supply is fragmented

Thrift sellers manage unique inventory with inconsistent size labels, weak metadata, and limited ability to match items to the right buyer.

02

Buyers lack fit confidence

Online shoppers cannot try items on, and brand size charts do not translate cleanly to secondhand inventory or to non-standard body types.

03

Waste compounds the pain

Bad fit reduces conversion, slows inventory turnover, increases returns, and pushes more apparel toward low-value clearance or disposal.

The missing layer is structured fit data.

BestFit treats measurement as a first-class domain, so body dimensions, garment dimensions, fit style, and tolerance rules can be compared consistently.

Why Kenya first

Thrifting is already mainstream, fashion discovery is increasingly digital, and fit uncertainty is one of the biggest blockers to scaling online resale.

The Solution

BestFit combines a thrift marketplace with a reusable fit intelligence layer.

The marketplace wedge creates liquidity and proprietary data. The measurement engine becomes the long-term platform.

1

Buyer profile

Create a structured body profile instead of relying on guessed sizes.

Measurements grouped by body region and unit system.

2

Seller capture

Upload garment photos plus actual dimensions for the specific item.

Especially valuable for one-off thrift inventory.

3

Normalization

Map both sides into consistent measurement entities and garment categories.

Eliminates brand-by-brand ambiguity.

4

Fit scoring

Rank items using fit style, garment relevance, and tolerance rules.

Accounts for elasticity and intended fit.

5

Higher confidence

Surface likely-fit items first, improving conversion and reducing waste.

Marketplace benefit now, API opportunity later.

Why BestFit Wins

The moat is not just inventory. It is the measurement system behind the transaction.

BestFit can evolve from a local marketplace into a fit infrastructure company because the core model is extensible across brands, garment types, and downstream applications.

Human body dimension model

  • Standard measurement definitions
  • How each measurement is taken
  • Anatomical references and unit handling
  • Inclusive treatment of body variation

Fit context layer

  • Measurement relevance by garment type
  • Fit style logic: slim, regular, oversized
  • Fabric elasticity and tolerance rules
  • Potential to learn from outcomes over time

Integration layer

  • Marketplace ranking and recommendations
  • Seller measurement tooling
  • Cross-brand fit APIs
  • Tailoring, scanning, and research partnerships
What others do

Most resale platforms optimize listing and logistics. Most sizing tools sit on top of brand size charts. Few treat measurement data as the product.

BestFit advantage

Capture the transaction and the fit layer together, so every matched purchase improves the measurement graph and strengthens the recommendation engine.
